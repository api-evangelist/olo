Every restaurant has a handful of guests who show up like clockwork—the same order, the same booth, a first-name greeting from the staff. While loyalty already exists, the harder part is building a program that makes guests feel recognized instead of training them to wait for the next discount. Really, it's hospitality at scale: taking the recognition a regular gets in person and making it work across every digital touchpoint.
