Restaurant leaders used to win on transactions: cover counts, same-store sales, the number on the P&L at month-end. Knowing who was dining with you, and why they kept coming back, wasn't the job of the finance team. That intel usually lived with a shift manager who remembered a regular's order, and it rarely made its way into a marketing plan or a real estate decision.
